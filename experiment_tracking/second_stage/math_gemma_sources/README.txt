Portable Gemma MATH-500 second-stage source prompts
===================================================

These files are Git-trackable copies of the five selected first-stage prompts.
They allow remote runs to avoid depending on ignored files under `outputs/`.

- rpo5.txt: RPO iteration 5.
- rpo10.txt: RPO iteration 10.
- evoprompt5.txt: experimental EvoPrompt-5, actual iteration 1.
- evoprompt10.txt: experimental EvoPrompt-10, actual iteration 2.
- etgpo1.txt: ETGPO iteration 1.
