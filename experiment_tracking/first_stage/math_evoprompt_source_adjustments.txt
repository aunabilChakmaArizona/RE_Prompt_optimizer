==================================================
Updated: 2026-09-16

Purpose
-------
Both models originally retained the same actual iteration-2 winner at the
iteration-5 and iteration-10 snapshots. For two distinct second-stage source
prompts, we assign actual iteration 1 to experimental EvoPrompt-5 and actual
iteration 2 to experimental EvoPrompt-10. These are experimental source-slot
names, NOT a claim that the prompts were produced at actual iterations 5 and
10. The original snapshot files and summaries remain unchanged.

Scores use the OpenAI/PRM800K grader. Stable = mean fold accuracy - 1 * fold
standard deviation. Compare gains within each run only; no test data were used.

Audit and reproducibility
-------------------------
- All four texts match the logged iteration-completed events exactly.
- The Qwen copies above are portable, Git-trackable source files because the
  original first-stage artifacts under `outputs/` are intentionally ignored.
- Each pair contains two distinct, complete prompts, not parser fragments.
- The Qwen higher prompt includes a boxed-answer suggestion; it is retained
  as actually generated, not manually edited. Its interaction with the fixed
  answer instruction should be kept visible during later evaluation.
- codes/report_first_stage_results.py preserves these source assignments when
  regenerating detailed_first_stage_results.txt and first_stage_summary_and_prompts.txt.
- Future second-stage scripts must use these explicit source files, not the
  original duplicate prompt_iteration_5.txt and prompt_iteration_10.txt pair.
